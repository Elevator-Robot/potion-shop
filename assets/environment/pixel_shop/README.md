# Interactive pixel shop

The current cottage uses `door.png` from this set. Floors/walls/windows and
shelves have moved to `assets/cottage/`. Chests are removed from play. This older
kit remains available as reference; the history below describes its earlier use.

The floor, masonry, windows, door and chests remain active. Shelf/floor-clutter
art has moved to `assets/furniture/library/`, item sprites to `assets/items/`,
and the cauldron to `assets/props/cauldron/`. The older stocked displays and item
atlas below are retained as reference, not used by the current shelf scene.

Original project artwork drawn at native integer pixel coordinates with a warm
wood/cool stone palette. Editable SVG frames and transparent PNG textures are
generated by `tools/draw_shop_interior.py` (Python 3 + ImageMagick). No external
asset pack or runtime SVG pattern support is required.

Rows and individual frame sources are retained beside the sheets. Regenerate:

```sh
python3 tools/draw_shop_interior.py
```

The floor and masonry are static pixel art; doors, chest lids, cauldron fire,
liquid, window glints and item sprites use actual animation frames. Accepted
inventory transfers fly between player and target; accepted storage/shelf actions
trigger the matching prop animation on both clients.

Furniture positions, approach points and convex ground footprints come from
`Game.FURNITURE` and the matching shape tables in `network/game.gd`.
All props, customers and players share world Y-sorting so people can walk behind
tall shelving. Baskets, book piles and loose-bottle groups also have collision
footprints. The door automatically
opens for arriving/departing customers and can also be operated with E.

## Furniture variety and contrast

`tools/shelf_art.py` draws the shelf collection: carved dark walnut, cherry
apothecary drawers, three-quarter teal and mahogany cabinets, a side-on birch
cabinet, an indigo sideboard, a moss-stained herb rack, low pine sorting bins,
and cream-painted specimen storage. Tops, side panels and shelf planes are
drawn in perspective instead of rotating a flat upright image. Wood grain,
deep cubby shadows, edge wear and brass/iron hardware separate them from the
quieter warm floor. Chests use a contrasting teal wood finish.

Stock artwork includes ferns, mushrooms, berries, blossoms, crystals, dried roots,
honey/specimen jars, flasks, spices, shells, scrolls, sacks, leaning books,
mortar/pestle and curios. These are visual variations; the playable recipe
ingredients remain Moonleaf, Firecap and Waterbloom.

The floor footprint is separate from the tall artwork above it. Angled and narrow
furniture uses corresponding convex collision; approaching an object follows its
open face. The same footprints drive local physics, full-segment host motion
validation, interaction line-of-sight, and customer navigation.

The generator overwrites its output. Preserve manual edits before regenerating.
